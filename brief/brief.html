One application.
Every form, completed.

A prospect answers a single branded application on any device. When they submit, Lexington's own credit application, certificate of exemption, and freight agreement come back filled in and signed, ready for the new account desk.

What the prospect sees

Welcome · choose designer or retailer
Freight · program rates and calculator
Review · one signature for every form

Why it matters

  • Nothing re-keyed. Company, contacts, ship-to hours, tax number, and freight election are typed once and land on each Lexington form in the right box.
  • Complete packets. The application will not submit without the entity type, a delivery schedule, a tax certificate on file, and a signature, so the new account desk stops chasing missing pages.
  • Freight and tax done right. The prepaid freight rate for the ship-to state, the fixed-value table, and the claims rules are shown before the customer agrees; the E-595E is completed with Lexington as seller.
  • No card data online. Trade accounts see how card details are collected by the credit department; the site never asks for a number.

What the rep and Lexington receive

Credit applicationDesigner (pay by card) or full net-30 version
E-595ECertificate of exemption, signed, with multistate page
Freight agreement2026 program rates, signed, when elected
CertificateThe customer's state resale document
SummaryOne-page record of every answer, plus data file
The rep reviews the packet, completes the internal checklist from the same data, and forwards to newaccount@lexington.com. Multiple ship-to locations attach automatically as an addendum.
